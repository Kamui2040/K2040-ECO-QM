### 1.0.0.9

## Previous project line

The earlier Nexus project `K2040's Eco Quick Menu Additions` (mod 104590) reached version **2.1.7** before being hidden and replaced by the current AiO and Single Patches projects. That predecessor included a broader and differently maintained patch set.

Historical archives from that project are treated as legacy files and are not part of the current 1.0 Single Patches release unless they were explicitly carried forward into the replacement project.
